How to play

  • Goal: move any Pokémon onto the opponent's goal (top for you if you're at the bottom; goals can be stood on, and some abilities forbid winning that way).
  • Your turn: (1) optionally use a Plate, (2) move ONE Pokémon up to its MP, (3) if you end next to an enemy, a battle happens. Battle ends the turn.
  • Entering: Pokémon start on the bench. Entering costs 1 MP and places them on one of your two free entry points (the ringed corners).
  • Battles: both wheels spin. White/Gold: higher damage wins, loser is KO'd. Purple effect beats White. Gold beats Purple. Blue blocks everything (including Gold) and knocks out a Miss. Purple vs Purple: more stars wins (tie = nothing). Red (miss) loses to everything but Purple.
  • Tagging: if your moved Pokémon ends next to your own Pokémon, their special conditions are removed.
  • KO: goes to your P.C. (holds 2). A third KO sends the oldest back to the bench with Wait 1. Surround an enemy on all its neighbouring points to KO it.
  • Wait N: can't act for N of its owner's turns. Statuses: poison −20, noxious −40 (both are cured when the Pokémon moves; MP markers come off when it moves or when an ally tags it), paralyzed/burned: smallest segment becomes a Miss, confused: spin lands on a neighbouring segment, asleep/frozen: can't move; defending, only Misses.
  • Z-Moves: each battle fills your gauge by 20%. When full, a Z-Move panel appears under the board: click a Pokémon that has a Z-Move, press its ⚡ button, then move it next to an enemy to attack with it. The gauge then resets.
  • Plates: 6 cost points of plates, each usable once per duel, only before moving.
